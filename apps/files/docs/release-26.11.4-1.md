# Shenzhen Files 26.11.4-1

- Friends (Beta): a Markdown document a friend sends from ShenzhenPDF arrives as a folder with its comments and pictures.

## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **Markdown documents arrive as folders.** When a friend sends you a Markdown document from ShenzhenPDF together with its comments or pictures, you get a folder named after it, such as "Report", where your received files go, holding the document, its comments and its pictures. The chat shows the document, "Report.md", and what comes with it, such as "With comments and 3 pictures", and **Show in Shenzhen Files** opens the folder. The same document sent again arrives as "Report (2)".

## Updating

Shenzhen Files updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
