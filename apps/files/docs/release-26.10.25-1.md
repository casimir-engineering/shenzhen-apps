# Shenzhen Files 26.10.25-1

- **Friends (Beta): attach any file** to a message, from your file picker.
- **Removing a friend and adding them again works.**
- **The right-click menu shows the Mac shortcuts,** such as ⌘C and ⌘V.
- **About shows the right version.**

## Friends (Beta)

- **Attach any file.** The paperclip beside **Send** opens your file picker, so you can send any file, several at once.
- **Adding someone again.** If you remove a friend and add them again, from Nearby or by their Friends ID, you are friends again at once and your messages go through. **Remove friend** takes you back to your list.

## Everyday browsing

- **Right-click menu.** Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All, New Folder, Properties and Move to Trash show their Mac shortcuts: ⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘A, ⇧⌘N, ⌘I and ⌘⌫.
- **About Shenzhen Files** shows the version you have, such as 26.10.25-1.

## Updating

Shenzhen Files updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
