# Shenzhen Files 26.11.11-1

- Friends (Beta): the You row shows how your other computers are connected.

## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **How your other computers are connected.** The You row now shows how your other computers are connected, as a friend's row does. If any of them goes through the Friends relay, it shows the relay. Point at the icon to see each computer and how it's connected.
- **Nothing connected.** When none of your other computers is connected, or you don't have another computer yet, the You row shows the crossed-out icon.

## Updating

Shenzhen Files updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
