## Friends (Beta)

Friends lets you send files and messages to people you add, straight from Shenzhen Files.

- **Add people.** Make yourself visible in Nearby so people around you can find you, or add someone by their Friends ID. They accept your request before anything is sent.
- **Send.** Drag files onto a friend, or onto the Friends button to choose who gets them. Write messages and see what was delivered. **Copy Download Link** gives you a link to a file for someone who does not use Friends.
- **Who receives it.** Your friend receives what you send while ShenzhenPDF, Shenzhen Files or Shenzhen 3D is open on their computer. All three apps share the same Friends, so you have one name, one ID and one list of friends on your Mac.
- **Open it.** Press **⌘J**, click the people button in the sidebar's title bar, or choose **View ▸ Friends (Beta)**.

**What beta means.** Friends works, but it is new. How it looks and what it can do may change in the next updates, and some parts may not be as smooth as the rest of Shenzhen Files yet. You can turn it off at any time in **Preferences ▸ Friends**, where you can also see what the Friends server sees: your network address, your Friends ID and when Shenzhen Files is open. It never sees your name, messages or files.

## Everyday browsing

- **⌘K** finds any command by a few letters of its name and runs it with Return; each command shows its shortcut.
- **Backspace** goes back, in the file list and in the sidebar.
- **Apps and archives.** Double-clicking an app opens it; archives offer Extract. Files dropped onto an app open with it, and app packages behave as single items.
- **Search.** The toolbar's search button searches the folder you are in. Delete edits a search you are typing.
- **Drives.** External disks, disk images and network volumes appear and disappear as you connect and eject them, including names written with accents.
- **Quick Look.** The keyboard stays in the Shenzhen Files window while a preview is open, and pausing a drag over an app no longer opens that app.

## Updating

Shenzhen Files updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
