# Shenzhen Files 26.11.4-2

- Friends (Beta) no longer gets stuck on "Starting Friends…" after an update.

## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **No more "Starting Friends…" after an update.** After Shenzhen Files, ShenzhenPDF or Shenzhen 3D updated itself, Friends could stay on "Starting Friends…". Friends now opens within a few seconds of the updated app starting, including right after this update.

## Updating

Shenzhen Files updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
