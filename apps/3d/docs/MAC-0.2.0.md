# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.0

- **Friends (Beta).** Add people nearby or by their ID, then send them models, projects and messages from Shenzhen 3D.
- **Trackpad.** A two-finger swipe zooms; hold ⌘ to pan.
- **Selecting parts** in the model tree highlights them without moving the view, and parts picked in the view are shown in the tree.
- **Large STEP files** open with much less memory.
- **Updates no longer need a GitHub account.**

## Friends (Beta)

Friends lets you send models, projects, files and messages to people you add, straight from Shenzhen 3D on the Mac.

- **Add people.** Make yourself visible in Nearby so people around you can find you, or add someone by their Friends ID. They accept your request before anything is sent.
- **Send.** Choose **Send project to a friend**, or drop files on the Friends button. Write messages and see what was delivered. **File ▸ Copy Download Link** gives you a link to the open model for someone who does not use Friends.
- **Who receives it.** Your friend receives what you send while ShenzhenPDF, Shenzhen Files or Shenzhen 3D is open on their computer. All three apps share the same Friends, so you have one name, one ID and one list of friends on your Mac.
- **Open it.** Press **⌘J**, click the people button, or choose **View ▸ Friends (Beta)**.

**What beta means.** Friends works, but it is new. How it looks and what it can do may change in the next updates, and some parts may not be as smooth as the rest of Shenzhen 3D yet. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, under **Enable Shenzhen Friends**, where you can also see what the Friends server sees: your network address, your Friends ID and when Shenzhen 3D is open. It never sees your name, messages or files.

## Viewing

- **Trackpad.** A two-finger swipe up or down zooms, centered in the view, like the mouse wheel. Hold ⌘ (Ctrl on other systems) and swipe to pan in any direction. Pinch still zooms and two-finger twist still rotates.
- **Selecting parts.** Choosing a part in the model tree highlights it without zooming or breaking the picture, and a part you pick in the view is selected in the tree.
- **Large STEP files.** Big assemblies are read product by product and repeated parts are shared, so files that used to run out of memory now open.
- **Ray tracing.** A finished trace now has the same brightness as the normal view; before, the light from the surroundings was about three times too strong.

## Updating

From this version, Shenzhen 3D finds and installs updates without a GitHub account. Version 0.1.9 and earlier still ask you to connect GitHub before they can check for this update: connect once in **Updates**, or download the DMG below and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
