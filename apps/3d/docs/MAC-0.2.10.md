# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.10

- Friends (Beta), on the Mac: your Wi-Fi no longer stutters while Friends runs, and a friend reached over Bluetooth stays connected.
- Friends (Beta): a finished file shows how it went through.

## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **Steadier Wi-Fi, on the Mac.** Friends kept your Mac's direct Wi-Fi link to nearby Macs open all the time. That made your Wi-Fi pause about twice a second, which slowed everything on it, including files sent to friends on the same network. Friends now opens that link only when it helps.
- **Bluetooth stays connected, on the Mac.** A friend reached over Bluetooth was disconnected every 15 minutes or so. The link now stays up.
- **Reconnecting after a friend restarts.** When a friend's Friends restarts, you're reconnected over Bluetooth within about half a minute, on every platform.
- **How a file went through.** A finished file keeps a thin bar in the route colours, showing how much of it went over your network, direct Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or the internet. Files sent before this version show no bar.

## Updating

- **Mac, Windows, Linux and Android.** Shenzhen 3D finds this update by itself and asks before installing it. With the Linux `.deb` or `.rpm`, it tells you the update is out and **Download** gets it for you to install.
- **iPhone and iPad.** Shenzhen 3D tells you when a new version is out. It installs only on the iPhones and iPads registered for testing.

## Downloads

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.10-Apple-Silicon.dmg`. Open it and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
- **Windows** (x64): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.10-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`, or the `.msi`. They are not signed, so Windows may warn you before it installs them.
- **Linux** (ARM64): the `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **Android** (ARM64): `shenzhen-3d-0.2.10-android-arm64-development.apk` installs directly; Android asks you to allow apps from this source first. The `.aab` is for publishing to a store and is not signed.
- **iPhone and iPad**: `shenzhen-3d-0.2.10-ios-registered-devices.ipa` installs only on the devices registered for testing. `shenzhen-3d-0.2.10-ios-arm64-simulator.tar.gz` runs in Xcode's iOS Simulator.
- **Browser**: unzip `shenzhen-3d-0.2.10-web.zip` and serve the folder over HTTP or HTTPS.

Friends (Beta) is in every version except the browser one.
