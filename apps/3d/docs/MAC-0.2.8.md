# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.8

- Friends (Beta): the You row shows how your other computers are connected, now on every platform.

## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **How your other computers are connected.** The You row now shows how your other computers are connected, as a friend's row does. If any of them goes through the Friends relay, it shows the relay. Point at the icon to see each computer and how it's connected.
- **Nothing connected.** When none of your other computers is connected, or you don't have another computer yet, the You row shows the crossed-out icon.

On the Mac, this came in 0.2.7; 0.2.8 brings it to Windows, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad.

## Updating

- **Mac, Windows, Linux and Android.** Shenzhen 3D finds this update by itself and asks before installing it. With the Linux `.deb` or `.rpm`, it tells you the update is out and **Download** gets it for you to install.
- **iPhone and iPad.** Shenzhen 3D tells you when a new version is out. It installs only on the iPhones and iPads registered for testing.

## Downloads

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.8-Apple-Silicon.dmg`. Open it and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
- **Windows** (x64): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.8-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`, or the `.msi`. They are not signed, so Windows may warn you before it installs them.
- **Linux** (ARM64): the `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **Android** (ARM64): `shenzhen-3d-0.2.8-android-arm64-development.apk` installs directly; Android asks you to allow apps from this source first. The `.aab` is for publishing to a store and is not signed.
- **iPhone and iPad**: `shenzhen-3d-0.2.8-ios-registered-devices.ipa` installs only on the devices registered for testing. `shenzhen-3d-0.2.8-ios-arm64-simulator.tar.gz` runs in Xcode's iOS Simulator.
- **Browser**: unzip `shenzhen-3d-0.2.8-web.zip` and serve the folder over HTTP or HTTPS.

Friends (Beta) is in every version except the browser one.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every file here.
