# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.3

- **The keyboard on Android** no longer covers what you type.
- **Attach any file** in Friends (Beta), from your file picker.
- **The header fits small phone screens.**
- **The right version number** shows in the app.

## Friends (Beta)

- **Attach any file.** The paperclip beside **Send** opens your file picker, so you can send any file, several at once. Typing **#** still picks the open model or a recent file.
- **Share from other apps on Android.** Choose **Send to a friend** when you share files from another app, then pick who receives them.

## Phones

- **The keyboard on Android** no longer covers the field you are typing in, and picking files to attach no longer brings the keyboard up.
- **The header fits small phone screens.** With a model open, every button stays on screen; the ones that do not fit move into a **More** menu.

## Everywhere

- **The right version number.** The app shows 0.2.3 at the bottom of the window and in **Updates**.

## Updating

- **Mac, Windows, Linux and Android.** Shenzhen 3D 0.2.2 finds this update by itself and asks before installing it. With the Linux `.deb` or `.rpm`, it tells you the update is out and **Download** gets it for you to install.
- **iPhone and iPad.** Shenzhen 3D 0.2.2 tells you when a new version is out. It installs only on the iPhones and iPads registered for testing.

## Downloads

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.3-Apple-Silicon.dmg`. Open it and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
- **Windows** (x64): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.3-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`, or the `.msi`. They are not signed, so Windows may warn you before it installs them.
- **Linux** (ARM64): the `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **Android** (ARM64): `shenzhen-3d-0.2.3-android-arm64-development.apk` installs directly; Android asks you to allow apps from this source first. The `.aab` is for publishing to a store and is not signed.
- **iPhone and iPad**: `shenzhen-3d-0.2.3-ios-registered-devices.ipa` installs only on the devices registered for testing. `shenzhen-3d-0.2.3-ios-arm64-simulator.tar.gz` runs in Xcode's iOS Simulator.
- **Browser**: unzip `shenzhen-3d-0.2.3-web.zip` and serve the folder over HTTP or HTTPS.

Friends (Beta) is in every version except the browser one.
