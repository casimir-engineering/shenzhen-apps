# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.2

- **Notifications** for messages, files and friend requests in Friends (Beta).
- **One profile on all your devices.** Your phone and your computers can share one name, one ID and one list of friends, with the files you received earlier.
- **Remove people** from your list of friends.
- **Received files go to Downloads**, and the models among them appear in your recent files.
- **Type # to send a recent file.**
- **Windows, Linux and Android now update by themselves.**

## Friends (Beta)

- **Notifications.** Shenzhen 3D tells you when a friend sends you a message or a file, when someone asks to be your friend, and when a computer or phone asks to join your profile. Clicking a notification opens the conversation. You can turn notifications off in Settings, under **Notifications**.
- **Your phone and your computers, one profile.** On a new computer or phone, open Friends and choose **Use my existing profile…**, then approve it on a device that already has your profile. Both then have the same name, ID, friends and conversations. A file you received on one of them can be fetched on another with **Get**, and **Get again** fetches a file you moved or deleted from another of your devices that still has it.
- **Removing people.** Right-click someone in your list, or press and hold on a phone, and choose **Remove friend**.
- **How people nearby were found.** Each person nearby says how your device sees them: on your network, over Bluetooth, or both.
- **Received files go to Downloads.** On a computer you can choose another folder in Settings, under **Save received files to**. On Android received files go to Downloads, and on iPhone and iPad to the Files app. Models you receive appear in your recent files, so you can open them later.
- **Type # in a conversation** to pick the open model or a recent file and send it.
- **Fixes** for finding people over Bluetooth and for connecting to friends and to your other devices.

## Phones

- **Full screen on Android, iPhone and iPad.** The app uses the whole screen, with its buttons clear of the status bar and the screen's edges.
- **The keyboard on Android** no longer covers the field you are typing in.

## Mac

- **A smaller download.** The Mac installer is about a fifth smaller.

## Updating

- **Mac.** Shenzhen 3D 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 find this update by themselves and install it when you choose **Restart & install**.
- **Windows and Linux.** If you have 0.2.0 or 0.2.1, download 0.2.2 below and install it by hand, once. From 0.2.2 on, Shenzhen 3D finds new versions by itself: **Update** downloads the new version and **Restart & install** installs it. With the `.deb` or `.rpm`, Shenzhen 3D tells you when a new version is out and **Download** gets it for you to install.
- **Android.** If you have 0.2.0, install the APK below by hand, once; your files, settings and friends stay. From 0.2.2 on, Shenzhen 3D finds new versions by itself and asks before installing them.
- **iPhone and iPad.** If you have 0.2.0, install the `.ipa` below by hand, once. From 0.2.2 on, Shenzhen 3D tells you when a new version is out. It installs only on the iPhones and iPads registered for testing.

## Downloads

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.2-Apple-Silicon.dmg`. Open it and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
- **Windows** (x64): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.2-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`, or the `.msi`. They are not signed, so Windows may warn you before it installs them.
- **Linux** (ARM64): the `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm`.
- **Android** (ARM64): `shenzhen-3d-0.2.2-android-arm64-development.apk` installs directly; Android asks you to allow apps from this source first. The `.aab` is for publishing to a store and is not signed.
- **iPhone and iPad**: `shenzhen-3d-0.2.2-ios-registered-devices.ipa` installs only on the devices registered for testing. `shenzhen-3d-0.2.2-ios-arm64-simulator.tar.gz` runs in Xcode's iOS Simulator.
- **Browser**: unzip `shenzhen-3d-0.2.2-web.zip` and serve the folder over HTTP or HTTPS.

Friends (Beta) is in every version except the browser one.
