# Shenzhen 3D 0.2.1

Shenzhen 3D works as in 0.2.0; nothing in the app has changed. [What is new in 0.2.0](MAC-0.2.0.md).

## Updating

Shenzhen 3D 0.2.0 finds this update by itself and installs it when you choose **Restart & install**, without a GitHub account. You can also download the DMG below and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.

## Downloads

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Shenzhen-3D-0.2.1-Apple-Silicon.dmg`. Open it and drag Shenzhen 3D into Applications.
- **Windows, Linux, Android, iPhone, iPad and the browser**: use [0.2.0](https://github.com/casimir-engineering/shenzhen-3D/releases/tag/preview-0.2.0), which is the same version for them.
