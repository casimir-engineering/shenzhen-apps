- Crowded tabs scroll freely; +N opens hidden-tab lists and other groups stay visible
- Saved image rotation, better first-press fitting and natural image colors in dark mode
- Delete individual History versions or clear previous backups; compare older versions with Latest
- Cleaner reader chrome, draggable panel headers and copyable full paths in Properties

### Opening documents and reader controls

- **A welcoming empty reader.** A centered document illustration, supported-format guide and drag-and-drop hint replace the bare message. Click **+ Open document** or use **Cmd+O**. The welcome view is created only for an empty canvas.
- **Reliable zoom and text-size buttons.** Zoom and text size each have a compact pair with a subtle background, separated by a clear gap. The page counter shares a consistent font and baseline; the zoom selector centers its label and sizes itself to its longest menu item. Pointer targets match the drawn segments, including their edges. Repeated clicks work immediately, and pressed feedback stays inside the clicked half.

### Tabs and groups

- **Use the whole tab lane.** Crowded groups scroll with a mouse wheel or a two-finger trackpad gesture. The left and right `+N` controls appear over the tabs only when needed, with tabs fading behind them. They reserve no blank space.
- **Choose a hidden document directly.** Click either `+N` to see the offscreen documents on that side, organized by group. Opening the list leaves the carousel and current document unchanged; choosing a document opens it. VoiceOver offers the same list action.
- **Keep other groups in sight.** When one group is expanded and space permits, the other collapsed group headings stay visible in their actual order around the scrolling document lane. If the headings themselves exceed the available width, the whole strip scrolls instead of silently dropping groups.
- **Resume the same tab-strip position.** Manual scrolling is saved per window and restored after relaunch. Refreshing the workspace leaves that position alone; selecting a different document brings its tab into view.
- **Compact group controls.** Group pills size to their names, with a small minimum width. Hovering reveals only a hide eye over fading text; there is no reserved action-button space or rename icon. Right-click **Rename Group…** opens the name editor anchored to the group. Clicking the group name still expands or collapses it without switching documents.
- **See and change visibility in All Groups.** The picker lists every group and its document count, including hidden groups. Visible groups have a normal eye; hidden groups have a dimmed barred eye. Click the eye to show or hide the group without closing its documents. There is no extra “Hidden” label. Both All Groups and the left Group Management panel offer a separate collapse/expand button for the tab-bar group. The right menu is wider so these controls do not reduce space for group names.

### Group Management

- **Keep browsing your groups.** Opening a document with a remembered search no longer switches Group Management to Search after a delay. Restored results stay in the background; an explicit search still opens the Search panel.

- **Select on press, open on release.** Document rows respond immediately to mouse-down. Starting a drag does not open the document. Monochrome extension badges (`.pdf`, `.md`, `.jpg`, and others) identify file types separately from document titles and remain stable during loading, and opening no longer performs a redundant group save and sidebar refresh first.
- **Reorder documents directly.** Drag between document rows to reorder within a group or move into another. Drop on a group heading to append. Groups stay expanded. Crowded lists start scrolling before the dragged document reaches the edge and accelerate as the pointer gets closer. Cancelling a drag leaves disclosure state usable.
- **Document actions where expected.** Right-click a document for the same actions as its tab, including **Close Document**. Tab-bar collapse controls use distinct inward/outward arrows; list disclosure keeps its chevrons.

- **More room for reading.** The zoom toolbar has less padding above and below, keeping text and controls at their existing sizes. Narrow-window wrapping and the map toggle follow the compact spacing.

### Images and fitting

- **Image-color controls work on OCR scans.** Full-page photos inside PDFs now honor the document’s image-color setting, including after OCR adds a searchable text layer. An older scan-specific override no longer forces inversion regardless of the toggle.

- **Choose image inversion per document.** The image button beside night mode and **Cmd+Shift+I** toggle image-color inversion in the dark reading theme for the current document. The choice survives tab restoration and closing/reopening the file. **Settings → Keep Image Colors in Dark Theme by Default** controls new documents only and has no shortcut; it does not change open documents or override their remembered choices.

- **Save image rotation.** **Cmd+R / Shift+Cmd+R** rotates writable images and saves their orientation so it survives reopening, just as PDF rotation does. JPEG rotation updates orientation without recompressing its pixels. If an image cannot be saved in its format, the app explains the failure and leaves the source intact.
- **Get the intended fit on the first press.** **Fit Page (Cmd+1), Fit Width (Cmd+2) and Fit Height (Cmd+3)** settle scrollbar changes before measuring. Switching between fit modes no longer leaves extra space at the bottom of an image, clips an edge, or requires pressing the same shortcut twice.
- **Preserve standalone image colors in dark mode.** With **Keep Image Colors in Dark Theme** enabled, standalone pictures retain their colors. Disable it to apply the dark reading theme. Actual image copies are recognized even when their saved filename has a generic extension. Printed and exported pages retain the document's own colors.

### Collection and History

- **Delete a particular saved version.** Right-click a saved version in the reader's **History** panel and choose **Delete version…**. The confirmation applies to that clicked version, even if another row is selected afterward. The original file is not deleted.
- **Clear earlier backups while keeping the latest.** On the linked original/latest History row, **Delete all previous backups…** removes older snapshots and retains the original, its link and the newest saved copy. Collection's former **Delete Selected Copies** command now has this same name and scope for the selected documents. If the original is missing, its latest saved copy can still be deleted as an individual version.
- **Compare an older version with Latest.** Right-click an older History version and choose **Compare with Latest**. The comparison uses that clicked snapshot. The latest version omits this self-comparison command, and Collection no longer offers it because its main list already shows the latest copy. **Compare with Previous** remains available where useful.
- **Content-hash storage remains shared.** Identical file content shares one stored object, and capturing unchanged content does not add a consecutive duplicate version. Returning to earlier content can still create a distinct point in the timeline while reusing the stored object. Deleting history releases objects only when no remaining version references them. Changed content uses full copies; this release adds neither compression nor delta storage.

### Reader layout and file information

- **Copy the complete file path.** **File → Properties** displays the full path on multiple lines when necessary. Select and copy the whole path or just a part of it.
- **Drag from panel headers.** Empty space around the tools at the top of the left panel and around the map controls at the top of the right panel can move the window. Tool buttons retain their own click targets.
- **More room for the document.** The bottom status bar is removed; page count and zoom remain in the top toolbar. A source-status pill appears at the bottom of the left panel only when relevant to a collected or working copy, rather than labelling ordinary originals.
- **Continuous panel dividers.** The separator lines reach the bottom edge without a visual break, while preserving the native window-resize area.

### For AI agents and configuration

- **Public control and authoring guide.** The main README links to the [AI control and Markdown authoring guide](../agent-interface.md). It documents CLI/MCP document inspection and navigation, persistent group operations, and Markdown output/layout conventions separately from the user-facing feature overview.
- **Faster bulk API grouping.** `create-group` validates the complete member list before changing it, then updates and saves the group once. It selects the last requested tab once instead of activating and rendering every member, avoiding long requests when organizing many open documents.
- **Persistent tab navigation.** Each window records its manual tab-strip offset in `session.yaml` at `windows[].sidebar.tabStripScroll`. It works with the existing group identity, order, visibility and collapse settings. Restore validates the value; scroll bursts coalesce into a single save. This release adds no new CLI/MCP command for the overflow lists.
- **Lazy work stays lazy.** Hidden-tab menus are created on request, not at launch or while scrolling. Image classification needed for preserving colors is deferred until the relevant dark-theme render. Document rendering continues to receive explicit theme/options rather than reading ambient interface state.

### Validation and updates

- Headless native tests cover crowded groups, bilateral lists, selecting a hidden tab, visibility controls, hover/hit targets, VoiceOver, YAML restoration, image fitting and colors, History deletion and comparison. Reader workspace probes exercise both themes and multiple window widths without opening or interrupting a user's app.
- The updater keeps the same repository, release asset name, pinned Developer ID and verification path. Its regression tests remain part of the release validation.

### AI control: organize a closed reader

- Group API and MCP commands now organize the saved tabs while ShenzhenPDF is closed. They reuse the UI group model and lock/atomically update session YAML without launching the app or reading document contents. The latest focused saved window is the default; `windowSessionID` selects another. Other windows, reading positions and unknown settings remain intact. Live commands continue to work while the app is open.
