## A new look

- **Calm greys.** The tab bar, side panel, assistant, Collection and Settings use quiet neutral greys, in light and dark. Icons are sharper and the same size along each toolbar.
- **Colourful tab groups.** Groups have new, brighter colours. To pick another set, open **Settings › Appearance**: Pastel but vibrant (the new default), Neon, Coolors vivid, Coolors pastel rainbow, Cyberpunk, Pastel, Apple system or Codex. Every window changes at once.
- **Light or Dark.** **Settings › Appearance** can keep ShenzhenPDF light or dark whatever your Mac uses, or follow your Mac as before. Pages keep their own reading theme.

## Assistant

- **Long tasks finish.** Big requests, such as sorting dozens of documents into groups, keep going until they are done instead of stopping after a few steps. If something is still left, the assistant says what it did and **Continue** picks up where it stopped.
- **Measures and makes files from your documents.** The assistant can work on your documents' pages and pictures in Python, to measure or calculate, and save what it makes, such as a table, a chart or a PDF report, to your Downloads folder. Documents it makes also go to Collection. Each one comes with a card to open it or show it in Finder.
- **Drop files or folders into a chat,** or add them with the new paperclip. The assistant can read them in that chat only; remove one and it can no longer read it.
- **Source links open the right page,** even after you have closed that document's tab. An answer drawn from one document shows a card with that page under it.
- **Rename any document.** Ask the assistant to rename documents, read-only ones included. When a file can't be renamed, the new name shows in its tab and in Collection, and stays after you reopen it. OCR, translation and PDF export now work on read-only documents too, and on documents that aren't on screen.
- **Knows when you last opened and viewed each document,** so you can ask it to close every document you haven't looked at for a week. One Undo brings them all back.

## Tabs

- **Page previews.** Hover a tab to see its current page under its full name, for every kind of document: PDF, Markdown, text, code, images and more. Hover a group's title to see its full name.
- **The selected tab comes fully into view,** clear of the counts (such as "+3") at the ends of the tab bar, and trackpad swipes on the tab bar scroll steadily, even when they are not quite straight.

## Chats

- **Back to the end when you send.** Sending a message takes the assistant's chat, or a Friends chat, to the end. If new messages arrive while you have scrolled up, a **New message ↓** button takes you to them.

## Opening, quitting and closing

- **Faster to open.** With a document to show, ShenzhenPDF opens about 30% sooner than 26.10.25-1.
- **Instant quit.** The window goes the moment you quit or close it; your work is saved as before.
- **Closing never asks.** Closing a pasted image no longer asks you to save it: it is already kept. Editing a read-only PDF no longer asks where to save a copy: ShenzhenPDF makes the copy and the tab moves to it.

## Reading

- **Find on huge documents.** Searching a document of thousands of pages no longer freezes the app. Matches show as they are found and you can step through them right away; the count climbs until the search is done.
- **The page field** is wide enough for every digit of the page count, so a page number you type is never cut off.
- **Markdown headings.** When a Markdown file changes on disk, its chapter list shows the new headings.
- **Links on web pages.** A website can link straight to a document in ShenzhenPDF, at a page or section. The page you asked for shows first while the rest downloads, and the document stays available offline.

## Friends (Beta)

- **# starts with what's on screen.** Type **#** in a message: the document on screen comes first, marked **Current**. Keep typing to narrow the list, then press Return to attach. While the list is open, Return never sends the message.
- **Paste to attach.** ⌘V in a message attaches files copied in Finder and pictures, such as a screenshot. Text still pastes as text.
- **Messages save on macOS 15 and earlier.** On those versions of macOS, Friends could not save messages, so sending and receiving failed. They work now.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
