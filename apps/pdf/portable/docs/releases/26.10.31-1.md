## Translate

- **Choose a language from the keyboard.** **Translate** opens a short list under the button. English is highlighted, so Return translates into English. Type to narrow the list (your recent languages come first), press Return to choose, then Return again to translate. Escape cancels.
- **On this Mac or AI.** A switch at the top of the list chooses where the translation is made: **On this Mac**, offline, in the languages it knows, or **AI**, in any language you type. Tab, ⌘1 and ⌘2 change it, and your choice is kept for next time.
- **Named after the language.** The translation is called "<name> (<Language>)", such as "Datasheet (French).pdf", and opens right after the original in its group.
- **Chapters kept.** A translated PDF keeps its chapters, including the ones ShenzhenPDF made for it, a Markdown document's headings and an EPUB's contents, with their titles translated.
- **Only the translation shows.** The original text no longer shows faintly under the translation, and copying or searching the translated PDF finds only the translated words.

## Documents

- **Move to Trash.** A tab's menu, the page's menu and Collection's list now have **Move to Trash**. ShenzhenPDF asks first, then the file goes to the Trash, where you can still put it back, and the document leaves your Collection. In Collection, select several documents to move them together.

## Collection

- **Page previews stay.** Collection keeps its page previews between openings, so they show at once instead of being drawn again, and scrolling stays smooth.
- **Closes instantly.** The window goes the moment you close it.

## Reading

- **The page you were on comes back.** If you quit right after turning a page, ShenzhenPDF opens on that page next time, even in documents whose pages have different sizes.

## Friends (Beta)

- **Faster transfers.** When your friend also has the latest ShenzhenPDF, Shenzhen Files or Shenzhen 3D:
  - files go over every connection you share at once, such as your network and Bluetooth. Bluetooth only ever helps: if it drops out, the file doesn't pause;
  - a connection that goes quiet is noticed within seconds, and the file carries on from exactly where it was;
  - **Get again** and copies between your own computers pick up where they stopped, even after a restart, and take the file from every device that has it, at once;
  - many small files sent together arrive many times faster.
- **Nothing sent twice.** A file a friend already sent you isn't sent again, and a new version of it brings only what changed.
- **Finishing….** After a large file arrives, its row says "Finishing…" while the file is checked, before it's saved.
- **Latest message in the list.** Each friend's row shows the latest message or file in your conversation, such as "Mei: how are you?" or "You: 📎 report.pdf", instead of how you're connected. The icons still show how a friend nearby is reached.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
