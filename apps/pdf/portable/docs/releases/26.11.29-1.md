## New in this version

### Annotate

- **One button for every document.** **Annotate** in the toolbar (⌥⌘F, or **Edit › Annotate**) opens a palette of tools over the document: on PDFs, pictures and new canvases. On a Markdown, text or other document, it first offers to save it as a PDF, which opens beside the original; the original is never changed.
- **Fill & Sign.** A PDF with a form opens ready to fill: fields are tinted, a click fills a field, ticks a box or opens a list, and Tab goes from one field to the next. On any PDF, typed text snaps to lines and boxes, and ✓ and ✗ marks land where you click. Draw or type your signature and initials, or take them from a picture; they are kept on this Mac. An empty box offers your details, today's date and texts you saved. **File › Save Final Copy…** saves a separate copy for sending, where what you filled in becomes part of the pages; your own file stays editable.
- **Paint tools.** Pencil, brushes, airbrush, eraser, colour eraser, fill and colour picker (Fill fills a shape or closed outline you drew, as an object you can move); rectangles, ovals, lines, arrows, curves and text; selections you can move, resize, turn, crop, flip, skew and invert. Each tool has its own size and opacity, and sliders take typed values.
- **On a PDF, the page stays as it is.** What you draw goes on top of the page and is saved into the PDF as annotations, so other PDF readers show it too; nothing erases or paints over the page itself. This also works on PDFs that open without a password but restrict changes, as many datasheets do.
- **On a picture.** The picture stays as it is under what you draw, as a PDF page does: brushes, the eraser and Fill work on the layers above it, so erasing never makes a hole in it. Crop, resize, rotate, flip and skew still change the whole picture. Saving writes the picture with your drawing in its own format; Collection keeps the version from before your first change. A WebP, camera RAW or animated picture is saved as a PNG copy instead.
- **Layers.** Each document starts with its base layer (the page or the picture) and Layer 1. Add layers, hide, lock or fade them, and right-click an object to move it to the layer above or below. The **Layers** panel lists each layer with its objects. A picture's layers come back when you open it again, and **File › Save As…** can keep them in a Shenzhen Paint project (.szpaint).
- **Paste and drop pictures** onto a page, a picture or a canvas: they land in view, ready to resize.
- **For scripts and agents.** `szpdf annotate` and `szpdf canvas new`, the assistant and the MCP server can use every Annotate tool.

### New Canvas

**File › New Canvas…** opens a searchable list of sizes: paper from A0 to A6, B4, B5, US Letter, Legal and Tabloid, screens, social media posts and icons, or your own size in pixels, millimetres, centimetres or inches. Choose portrait or landscape and a resolution in ppi; pictures saved from the canvas keep it. A new canvas is transparent and is saved as a PNG. **File › New Canvas from Clipboard** starts one from the picture you copied, at its size.

### Pictures

- **Text in pictures.** ShenzhenPDF reads the text in a picture on your Mac, in the background, so you can select, copy and search it as in a scanned PDF. **OCR** now asks who reads the text: **Open source** (OCRmyPDF, as before) or **macOS**, which reads only the pages that have no text yet.
- **Camera RAW photos** from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Olympus, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Leica, Pentax, Samsung, Hasselblad, Phase One and Sigma cameras, and DNG files, open upright and in the camera's colours.
- **Raw sensor frames** (`.raw`, `.raw10`) straight from an image sensor open as pictures; their context menu sets the Bayer order.

### Friends (Beta)

- **Download here.** A file on one of your computers, one you sent to You there or one a friend sent there, still shows on your other computers right away, but it no longer copies to them by itself. Choose **Download here** on each computer where you want it. On the computer that has the file, it says whether your other computers can download it, shows the progress while one of them does, and says **On all your computers** once they all have it. A copy that was under way when you update waits for **Download here**, then carries on from where it stopped. Your computers that haven't updated yet still copy files by themselves.
- **Faster transfers.** Sending and receiving take less of your Mac's processor and wait less for the disk, so files move faster over a fast network or a cable. The gain is largest with friends who have updated too.
- **Faster Bluetooth with Android phones.** Between your Mac and an Android phone running Shenzhen 3D, files over Bluetooth now go several times faster, in both directions. Friends now says **Bluetooth LE** wherever it names this connection.
- **Direct Wi-Fi only when you're off Wi-Fi.** While your Mac is on a Wi-Fi network, Friends no longer opens a direct Wi-Fi link beside it, which slowed that network. A file to a friend on the same Wi-Fi now goes over the network alone, at its full speed. Friends uses direct Wi-Fi to a nearby Mac or phone only when neither of you is on a Wi-Fi network, for example when both are on a cable, and only with friends who have updated too.
- **Direct connections through a VPN.** While your Mac uses a VPN, Friends can now connect directly to friends and to your other devices over the internet, instead of going through the much slower relay. To do this, that connection goes around your VPN, so the friend, and the Friends server that helps you connect, see your network's own internet address. If your VPN doesn't allow anything to go around it, Friends connects through the VPN as before.

### Tabs

- When you open ShenzhenPDF, the Groups panel shows every group open, with the document you're reading in the middle.

### Fixes

- Saving changes twice to a PDF that stays open, such as adding two comments, no longer damages the file.
- A new window opens at the size you left it, instead of a little shorter each time.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
