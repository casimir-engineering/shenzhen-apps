## New in this version

### Windows

- **Windows reopen where you left them.** When ShenzhenPDF reopens, after quitting, an update or an install, each window appears directly on the display and at the place you left it. Before, a window could open on another display, often the laptop's, and stay there.
- **A display that goes away.** A window that had to leave a display that was disconnected or went to sleep goes back once that display returns.

### Reading

- **No faint lines when scrolling.** Scrolling no longer leaves faint horizontal lines on the pages or on the dark background beside them, most often seen in Markdown documents at zooms such as Fit Page.

### Translate

- **Asks when it can't tell.** When you translate on this Mac and the document's language can't be recognised, for example because it is mostly numbers or part codes or very short, ShenzhenPDF asks which language it is in, with the one you chose last time selected. Cancel stops the translation.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
