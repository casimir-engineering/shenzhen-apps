## Friends (Beta)

- **Attach any file.** The paperclip beside **Send** opens your file picker, so you can send any file, several at once.
- **Adding someone again.** If you remove a friend and add them again, from Nearby or by their Friends ID, you are friends again at once and your messages go through. **Remove friend** takes you back to your list.

## Search

- **⌘K.** Each row fits the window, with its menu path and shortcut in view.

## Version

- **About ShenzhenPDF** and the update window show the version you have, such as 26.10.25-1.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
