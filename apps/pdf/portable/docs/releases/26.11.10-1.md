## New in this version

### Documents from other apps

- **No more prompt at every start.** A document you open from WeChat, Mail, Messages or Safari is now kept as ShenzhenPDF's own copy, under the same name, and its tab, recent items and Collection use that copy. So macOS no longer asks whether ShenzhenPDF may "access data from other apps" each time it starts.
- **When macOS may still ask.** It can still ask while you're looking at such a document, when ShenzhenPDF checks whether the other app has a newer version of it. If you don't allow it, you keep reading the copy.
- **A document that can't be read.** If a tab has no copy and the document can't be read, the tab tells you to open the document again from that app.

### Friends (Beta)

- **How your other computers are connected.** The You row now shows how your other computers are connected, as a friend's row does. If any of them goes through the Friends relay, it shows the relay. Point at the icon to see each computer and how it's connected.
- **Nothing connected.** When none of your other computers is connected, or you don't have another computer yet, the You row shows the crossed-out icon.
- **Your computers.** In your profile, Your computers shows the same under each computer.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
