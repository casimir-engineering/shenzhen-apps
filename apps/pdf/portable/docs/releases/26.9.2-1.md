- Flowcharts are laid out properly: ranked layers, routed edges, a fan that never crosses itself, labels that fit their shapes
- Diagrams, tables and images are sized to the page, and Rotate turns Markdown paper landscape
- Faster launch: the first page is prerendered for the document you actually opened
- A copy button on every code block, and every tab has a visible outline

---

- Mermaid flowcharts are laid out with a real layered algorithm instead of a
  grid guess: nodes are ranked, long edges are routed around the ranks they
  skip, connectors are curved, and the edges leaving one node are routed as a
  family so they cannot cross each other. `classDef` and `:::` styling,
  `<br/>` line breaks, HTML entities and `<-->` arrows render. A label is now
  fitted to the shape that is actually drawn — a stadium, diamond or
  parallelogram has less room than its bounding box — so text no longer
  overlaps the border.
- Diagrams are sized by the destination page rather than the fixed inline-image
  budget, so a wide power tree fills an A4 sheet and grows further on landscape
  paper; labels scale with it. Tables and images get the same page budget. A
  diagram whose reflow attempt overran the size budget is re-laid-out at its
  best fit instead of being drawn past the bottom of the box it reported.
- The Rotate commands work on Markdown: they turn the paper from portrait to
  landscape and back. The text stays upright; Save as PDF, Print and Copy Page
  follow the orientation on screen.
- Launch is faster. A Finder double-click delivers the file after the app has
  already started speculating on the previous session's document, so the
  launch prerender was thrown away on the most common launch of all; it now
  retargets to the document you opened. The outline and per-page comments are
  loaded off the main thread for both a named document and a session restore,
  a Launch Services lookup left the pre-paint path, the toolbar is laid out once
  before the first frame instead of nine times, and a first page whose
  background render is nearly done is waited for rather than rendered again.
  Measured: 398 → 358 ms to first paint on a 14 MB, 891-page datasheet; a
  13-tab restore settles at ~210 ms with no more 120 ms outliers.
- Every code block has a copy button to the left of its language picker.
- Tabs have a visible outline. An unselected tab was drawn with no border at
  all, over a background the same colour as the tab strip behind it, so in both
  the light and dark themes it had no edge you could see. Unselected tabs get a
  neutral hairline; the selected tab keeps its heavier accent border.
