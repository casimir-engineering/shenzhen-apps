## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **A Markdown document arrives as a folder.** When you send a Markdown document that has comments, or pictures and files from its own folder, your friend gets a folder named after it, such as "Report", holding the document, its comments and its pictures. Both chats show the document, "Report.md", and what comes with it, such as "With comments and 3 pictures". **Open** opens the document with its comments and pictures; **Show in Finder** shows the folder. The same document sent again arrives as "Report (2)".
- **Friends on an older version** get "Report.zip", which opens into the same folder in Finder.

### Collection

- **Comments and linked files are kept.** Collection keeps a Markdown document's comments and the files it links to, such as a PDF, with the document, as it already kept its pictures. Opened from Collection, for example after the original was moved or deleted, the document shows its comments and pictures, and comments you add there are kept.
- **Documents already in Collection** pick up their comments and linked files the next time you open them, if those are still beside the original.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
