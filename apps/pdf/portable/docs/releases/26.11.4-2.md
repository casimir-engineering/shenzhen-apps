## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **No more "Starting Friends…" after an update.** After ShenzhenPDF, Shenzhen Files or Shenzhen 3D updated itself, Friends could stay on "Starting Friends…", and **Try Again** didn't help. Friends now opens within a few seconds of the updated app starting, including right after this update.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
