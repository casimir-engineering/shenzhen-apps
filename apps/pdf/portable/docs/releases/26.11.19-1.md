## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **Steadier Wi-Fi.** Friends kept your Mac's direct Wi-Fi link to nearby Macs open all the time. That made your Wi-Fi pause about twice a second, which slowed everything on it, including files sent to friends on the same network. Friends now opens that link only when it helps: when you and a friend share no network, or when a file to a nearby Mac would go faster over it.
- **Direct Wi-Fi beside a slow network.** When your shared network is slow, a file to a friend's Mac nearby can now also go over a direct Wi-Fi link between the two Macs. If that doesn't make it faster, Friends closes the link again.
- **Bluetooth stays connected.** A friend reached over Bluetooth was disconnected every 15 minutes or so. The link now stays up, and when a friend's Friends restarts, you're reconnected over Bluetooth sooner.
- **How a file went through.** A finished file keeps a thin bar in the route colours, showing how much of it went over your network, direct Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or the internet. Files sent before this version show no bar.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
