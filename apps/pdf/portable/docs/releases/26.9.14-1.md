- OCR reads dimensions on technical drawings, and no longer refuses tagged scans
- Zoom to 1600%

---

- **Translate a whole Markdown document.** It is rendered to a PDF beside it, and that is translated to `<name>_<lang>.pdf`. Translating a selection is unchanged.
- **OCR reads technical drawings.** Dimension numbers sitting among line art were being discarded; they are recognised now.
- **OCR no longer refuses a tagged scan.** A scanned PDF marked as coming from an office document used to stop before the first page.
- **OCR re-reads a scanned page** instead of trusting the text layer a previous pass left on it.
- **OCR and Translate install into Shenzhen PDF's own Python environment**, never onto the machine. A conda, pyenv or virtualenv setup can no longer break them, and the installer shows what it is doing.
- **Zoom goes to 1600%.**
- **Markdown pages land flush with the top** of the window when you step through them with the arrow keys or Option+scroll.
- **Recently Opened keeps 25 documents**, up from 10.
