- Keep Image Colors is now per document, and defaults to on
- Dark theme: a PDF page has a visible edge again after switching tabs
- Diagrams no longer vanish when their layout runs out of time
- The minimap follows the reading theme, with clearer page separation

---

- *Settings ▸ Keep Image Colors in Dark Theme* belongs to the document you
  are reading rather than to the whole app. A datasheet whose figures are
  colour-coded can keep them while the scan in the next tab is darkened whole.
  It defaults to on, each document remembers its own choice across restarts and
  when you drag a tab into its own window, and a document you have never set
  takes your existing preference.
- In the dark reading theme, a PDF page had no visible edge. Switching tabs
  builds a fresh document view, and that view was not being given the reading
  theme: the area around the page fell back to a colour two levels away from
  the page itself, and the thin border that separates a dark page from its
  background was not drawn at all. Only the very first document opened after
  launch looked right. Both are fixed, and they hold across tab switches.
- The area around a dark page is darker, and the page border stronger, so a
  page reads as a sheet even where its margin is blank.
- The minimap now follows the reading theme: its background is the same dark
  gutter as the document, each page thumbnail sits on the theme's own paper,
  and every page has a visible outline instead of dissolving into one dark
  column.
- A flowchart whose layout ran out of time could disappear entirely rather
  than being drawn at its best fit. Diagrams are drawn under a time budget so a
  pathological figure cannot stall a page; when that budget expired mid-search
  the diagram was dropped. It is now laid out at its best fit instead.
