- Copy, search and translate text in any PDF; no "Copying is not allowed"
- Photos and figures keep their own colors in the dark theme, now on by default
- Links inside a PDF follow instantly and land at the top of the target page

---

### Copying is always allowed

- A PDF that asks viewers to forbid copying no longer stops you selecting,
  copying, translating or searching its text: the request is advisory, and the
  page is decrypted and in front of you either way. The "Copying is not
  allowed" dialog is gone.
- Printing and editing still follow the document's own flags.

### Keep Image Colors in Dark Theme defaults to on

- Photographs, screenshots and figures keep their own colors on a dark page
  instead of being darkened with it; the page around them still is. If you had
  already turned the setting off, it stays off.
- A page that is essentially one big image is a scan, and is still darkened
  whole so dark mode is not a no-op on it.

### In-PDF links follow instantly, at the top of the page

- Clicking a table-of-contents entry used to wait about half a second before
  moving, and then arrive centred — hanging half a viewport of the preceding
  page above the thing you asked to see. It now moves in about eleven
  milliseconds and top-aligns, honouring a named destination's own position
  down the target page and falling back to the page's start.
- The wait existed so a double-click on link text could select a word instead
  of jumping. A jump inside the document is only a scroll, so it is taken
  immediately and a following double-click puts the document back and selects
  the word under the pointer.
- A link that opens another application keeps the wait, deliberately: a second
  click can cancel it, and opening an app is not something a click can undo.
- Dragging across link text still selects it.
