## New in this version

### Comments

- **⌘J adds a comment.** **Edit ▸ Add Comment…** is now ⌘J: select some text and press it. Friends, which used ⌘J, is now ⌘; (see below).
- **Write in a box beside the text.** Add Comment and Edit Comment open a small box next to the passage instead of a dialog. Return starts a new line and ⌘↩ saves. Esc or Cancel throws the text away. A click outside closes the box but keeps what you typed, and it comes back the next time you comment on that passage.
- **Comments on Markdown documents.** Select some text in a Markdown document and choose **Add Comment…** (with nothing selected, the comment goes on the paragraph you clicked). The commented words are highlighted, the comment shows when you point at them, and the Comments panel lists them in order. They're saved beside the document in "<title> - comments.md", a file any Markdown viewer can read. Comments whose text was later deleted are listed under "Unplaced". **Copy Comments File** is in the page and tab menus, and **Copy Document and Comments** copies both files.
- **Comment cards.** The Comments panel shows each comment as a card: the author, the date and page, the passage it's on and the whole comment, which no longer runs into the next one. Click a card to jump to its passage. Point at a card to edit, delete or resolve the comment.
- **Replies and Resolve.** Replies, such as an AI agent's answer, show under their comment, in PDFs and Markdown. **Resolve** marks a comment done: its card fades and says "Resolved". **Reopen** brings it back.
- **Change your name.** Point at your name in the comment box, or on one of your own comment cards, and click the pencil to change the name new comments are signed with.
- **Comments on PDFs you can't change in place.** Adding or editing a comment on a read-only PDF, or one in a temporary folder, now works. Before, Edit Comment did nothing and Add Comment lost your selection.

### Versions

- **New Version.** **File ▸ New Version…** (also in the tab menu) keeps the document as it is now as v1, with its comments, and the file carries on as v2. Open comments come along to v2 unless you untick that option. Earlier versions are saved next to the document, such as "Report v1.md".
- **Versions.** Once a document has more than one version, **Versions** in the File and tab menus opens any of them, and **Compare v1 with** shows two side by side with their changes.
- **Kept for you.** When another app, such as an AI agent, rewrites a document you've commented on, ShenzhenPDF first keeps the old text as the previous version with your comments. Your open comments move to the new text. To turn this off, go to **Settings ▸ Reading & access**.

### Reading

- **Presentation mode zooms.** Pinch, ⌘+ and ⌘−, or ⌘-scroll to zoom in on the presented page. Scroll, use the arrow keys or drag to move around it. ⌘1 to ⌘4 fit the page, width, height or show it at 100%, as in the reader. ⌘R and ⌘⇧R rotate it. When you leave, the reader is back exactly as it was.
- **Fit Width and 100% keep your place.** They no longer jump to the bottom or top of the page: what was in the middle of the window stays there.
- **Rewritten Markdown shows.** A Markdown document that another app changes after you've switched away from its tab and back now shows the new text.

### Markdown pictures

- **Pictures fit.** A picture in a table cell fits its column instead of running off the page, and pictures in lists and quotes stay inside the margins. Small pictures keep their own size.
- **No missing pictures.** A document with many large pictures shows all of them. Before, some could show as "[Image: …]" instead.
- **SVG charts.** SVG pictures, such as matplotlib charts, look as they do in a browser: the right fonts, labels and legends that don't overlap, no black shapes. SVG and PDF pictures stay sharp at any zoom, in presentation mode and in print. In an exported PDF the chart's text can be selected, copied and searched.

### Tabs

- **New documents join the group you're looking at.** A document you open goes into the tab group shown in the tab bar, not into General.
- **Compare two documents.** Right-click another tab and choose **Compare to Current Document** to see the two side by side with their differences marked.

### Friends (Beta)

- **⌘; opens Friends.** It used to be ⌘J, which now adds a comment.
- **Use the keyboard.** Up and Down move through your friends while you type in the search field, and Return opens one. Escape clears the search, then closes Friends. Tab and Shift-Tab move through the list and the buttons. In a chat, ⌘[ goes back to the list.
- **Chats open at once.** Clicking a friend shows your conversation straight away.
- **Never stuck starting.** Friends no longer stays on "Starting Friends…" for good. If it can't start, it says so and offers **Try Again**.

### Command line

- **szpdf.** Install it from **Settings ▸ General ▸ Install Command-Line Tool**. Scripts and AI agents can use it to open documents at a page or heading, take pictures of pages, read, add, answer and resolve comments, keep and compare versions, and check how a Markdown file will look. It works behind your other windows and never brings ShenzhenPDF to the front unless asked. `szpdf help` explains each command.

### Quitting

- **Quitting always finishes.** Quitting from the Dock or by logging out right after copying something from ShenzhenPDF could leave it running with no windows. It now always quits.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
