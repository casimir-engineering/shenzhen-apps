## New in this version

### Friends (Beta)

- **Cancel stops the file on both computers.** When you cancel a file you're sending or receiving, your friend's computer now stops too, and the file shows as cancelled on both sides.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
