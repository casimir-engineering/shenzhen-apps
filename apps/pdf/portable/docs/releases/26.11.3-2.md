## New in this version

### Assistant

- **It no longer stops partway.** Clicking a chat in the chat list, even the one already open, or switching to another chat could stop the answer being written, and nothing said so. Now a chat keeps working while you read another one or start a new one.
- **One chat works at a time.** If you send a message while another chat is still working, your message stays in the box, ShenzhenPDF says which chat is busy, and **Show that chat** takes you there. Send it again once that chat is done.
- **Continue.** When the AI service has a passing problem, the assistant tries again by itself. If it still can't finish, or something stops it partway (for example, your account runs out of credits), it says what happened and offers **Continue**, which picks up where it stopped. A chat left unfinished, for example because ShenzhenPDF quit in the middle of an answer or the previous version stopped it, offers Continue when you open it.
- **See what's new in the chat list.** A blue dot marks a chat that got an answer while you weren't looking at it, and goes away once you've seen it. "· working" follows the date while a chat is working.

### Voice input (macOS 26)

- **Speak instead of typing.** Click the microphone beside **Send**, or press ⌥⌘V, and talk: your words appear in the message box as you speak, at the cursor, keeping what's already there. It never sends by itself. Click the microphone again, or press ⌥⌘V, Return or Esc, to stop; check the text, then press Return to send. One Undo takes back everything it heard.
- **Your language.** It listens in your keyboard's language. Right-click the microphone to choose another one; ShenzhenPDF remembers it. The first time, macOS asks to use the microphone, and a language may need a one-time download.
- **Stays on your Mac.** What you say is turned into text on your Mac. Only the message you send goes to the assistant.
- On macOS versions before 26 the microphone isn't shown.

### Friends (Beta)

- **Markdown documents arrive with their comments and pictures.** When you send a Markdown document that has comments, or pictures and files from its own folder, your friend gets one .zip named after it, such as "Report (with comments).zip" ("Report.zip" when it has no comments). Clicking **Open** on it in this version of ShenzhenPDF opens the document with its comments and pictures. It works however you send the document, and with **Copy Download Link**. Before, only the document went: no comments, and its pictures were missing.
- **Friends on an older version** get the same .zip. They can choose **Show in Finder** and open the .zip there: it becomes a folder holding the document, its comments and its pictures.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
