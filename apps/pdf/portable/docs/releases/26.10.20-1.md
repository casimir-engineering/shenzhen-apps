## Friends (Beta)

Friends lets you send documents, files and messages to people you add, straight from ShenzhenPDF.

- **Add people.** Make yourself visible in Nearby so people around you can find you, or add someone by their Friends ID. They accept your request before anything is sent.
- **Send.** Drop a document on a friend, or on the Friends button to choose who gets it. Write messages, follow what was delivered, and find a conversation again by searching.
- **Who receives it.** Your friend receives what you send while ShenzhenPDF, Shenzhen Files or Shenzhen 3D is open on their computer. All three apps share the same Friends, so you have one name, one ID and one list of friends on your Mac.
- **Open it.** Press **⌘J**, click the people button in the title bar, or choose **View ▸ Friends (Beta)**.

**What beta means.** Friends works, but it is new. How it looks and what it can do may change in the next updates, and some parts may not be as smooth as the rest of ShenzhenPDF yet. You can turn it off at any time in **Settings ▸ Friends**, where you can also see what the Friends server sees: your network address, your Friends ID and when ShenzhenPDF is open. It never sees your name, messages or files.

## Document assistant

- **More it can do, when you allow it.** In **Settings ▸ AI settings** you can let the assistant search the web, run Python to calculate and make charts, and send up to four helpers to look things up at the same time. Each switch says what leaves your Mac and what it costs in OpenRouter credits. Python runs cut off from the internet and from your files.
- **Longer documents and chats.** The assistant reads far more of a document before answering, and summarizes a long chat instead of stopping when it no longer fits.
- **Edit a question.** Change a question you already asked and the answer starts again from your edit. A reply keeps running while you edit, and a summary made meanwhile keeps your edit.
- **Links that go where they say.** File names in answers open the document; names that are really websites open in your browser. Opening a document from a chat brings its tab forward in its own group.
- **OpenRouter sign-in.** Connect OpenRouter stays clickable while the browser sign-in is open, and coming back to the app no longer cancels a sign-in that is still going.

## Search and reading

- **⌘K.** Return opens the highlighted row; the highlight follows the best match until you move it, and Return does nothing when there is nothing to choose. Each document is listed once, by its name.
- **Faster pages.** The pages you can see are drawn side by side after you open a document, jump or finish scrolling, and minimap thumbnails fill in as their pages render.
- **Translation choice.** Choose between translating on your Mac and translating with OpenRouter.

## Updating

ShenzhenPDF updates itself as before: it checks once a day, or when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu.
