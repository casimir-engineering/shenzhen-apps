- Underline and highlight render in Markdown, in tables and in diagram labels

---

- `<u>` and `<ins>` never rendered: an underline written in a Markdown document lost its markup and drew as ordinary text. It underlines now — in body text, in table cells, and combined with bold or italic.
- `<mark>` renders as a highlight, using the reading theme's own colour in light and in dark.
- Diagram labels understood only bold and italic. `<u>`/`<ins>` and `<s>`/`<strike>`/`<del>` now underline and strike through inside mermaid, js-sequence and flowchart.js labels, on screen and in every export.
