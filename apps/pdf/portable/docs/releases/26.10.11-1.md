- Organize saved chats in folders; search, resume and rename conversations.

## For readers

### Document chat

- **Chat beside the document.** Press **⌘K**, then **Return** with an empty search field to open the chat panel. The **Chats** icon opens saved conversations in the left sidebar. Selecting a chat shows it in a resizable panel while leaving the document visible. Hide and reopen the panel without losing the conversation or draft; its width is remembered.
- **Send and follow progress.** Return sends; Shift-Return adds a line. Thinking, active tools, pauses, errors and completion are shown explicitly. **Stop** cancels pending work. With a nonempty ⌘K search, Return still opens the selected document; **Ask** starts a chat with the query as an editable draft.
- **Consistent panel controls.** Chat actions align with the reader toolbar, Hide comes first, and Chats displays its selected state. Empty chats have no blank message block. Keyboard handling preserves IME composition, Escape, and text-versus-action Undo.
- **Readable conversations.** User messages have a muted blue background and assistant messages a neutral one, in both themes. Messages run oldest to newest, with new replies beside the composer at the bottom. Reopening and resizing preserve message contents; loading earlier messages keeps the reading position. Blank intermediate tool messages are omitted.
- **Clickable sources.** Cited passages open the document at the right page and highlight the retrieved text. Visual sources can link to a page or preview instead. Citation navigation waits for loading and handles restored tab positions on the first click without closing chat. Changed or unavailable evidence is reported instead of silently opening a different source.
- **Brief answer cards.** A supported number, range or short phrase can appear in a compact pastel card with a descriptive label, large answer and **View evidence** button. Cards adapt to narrow panels, remain selectable and searchable, and survive reopening. The evidence action is exposed to accessibility tools as well as mouse and keyboard input. Answers that need more qualification can use ordinary prose.
- **Page images and your current view.** An image-capable model can inspect full pages or detailed crops, with previews retained in chat. You can ask it to look at the visible document area, including the current zoom, selection and highlights, without moving your reading position. This captures document content only, not other applications.
- **Brief replies by default.** The assistant is instructed to answer the actual question in 1–3 short cited sentences unless asked for more. It chooses text or rendered pages according to the evidence needed. This is guidance, not a guarantee of correctness; models can still misinterpret a document or drawing.

### Saved chats and reversible actions

- **History with folders.** Chats have concise generated titles and timestamps. **General** is the default folder. Create folders, drag chats into them, or use the context menu to move them. Rename chats and folders directly in their rows without an animated dialog. Removing a folder keeps its conversations in General; custom names and collapsed states persist.
- **Two distinct searches.** Sidebar search finds chat titles and messages, showing highlighted context for message matches. **Search this chat** finds the exact passage in the current conversation and scrolls to it with a highlight. Return advances and Shift-Return goes back. Opening a history result also reveals its matching passage.
- **Organize through chat.** Ask the assistant to organize saved chats into folders, create or rename tab groups, move tabs, change supported reader controls, rename eligible documents, or work with supported PDF comments. Collection working-copy renames preserve the source reference, group and history.
- **Undo and Redo.** Supported assistant changes provide guarded Undo/Redo. From an empty composer, ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z operate those actions; typing retains ordinary text Undo. Undo refuses to overwrite subsequent user or external edits. The action journal lasts for the reader session; chats remain saved across launches. Native menu commands retain their own dialogs and do not acquire generic Undo support.
- **Document jobs.** The assistant can create a separate PDF export, OCR copy or translated copy in **Assistant Exports**, leaving the source unchanged. Stop cancels running work. Undo removes a generated output only while it still matches the recorded result. OCR and translation require their local tools to be installed already; encrypted PDFs require an unprotected copy, and scanned text needs OCR before translation.
- **Reliable state and recovery.** Sending no longer loses the user's message when the composer clears. Concurrent saves protect newer conversations. Truncated responses get one bounded retry; incomplete tool actions are not executed. A successful final action does not become an Error solely because the tool-step limit was reached. Chat deletion asks for confirmation and removes transcripts and previews, not documents.

### Setup, language and document metadata

- **Four-step setup.** The first normal launch after upgrading to this setup experience walks through **Full Disk Access**, **Collection**, **OpenRouter**, and **Default reader**. Progress is saved. Full Disk Access is optional, there is no Accessibility step, and **Done** returns to the reader. Completed setup does not reopen on every launch.
- **Connect and choose a model.** Connect to OpenRouter in a browser, or reveal an API-key field when requested. Model selection uses a searchable catalog of tool-capable models; **Images** marks those that accept page images. DeepSeek remains the default model, subject to catalog availability. Models are never silently switched.
- **Keychain credentials.** Keys stay in macOS Keychain. Startup/background reads do not display password prompts. If access is denied, chat retains the draft and offers **Authorize & send…** or reconnection; only an explicit authorization action can open the system prompt.
- **Language preferences.** A searchable language picker in setup and AI settings controls default replies and generated summaries, tags and chapters. A language requested in chat overrides that reply without changing the saved default.
- **One analysis request per treatment.** An eligible opened document gets a short summary, keywords, up to six tags and suggested missing chapters together. Authored bookmarks and Markdown headings remain authoritative. Generated chapters use plain descriptive titles without emoji. Cached metadata is visible in **File → Properties**.
- **Latest revisions only.** Historical Collection previews and working copies based on older revisions do not get separate automatic summaries. Compatible new revisions reuse prior analysis plus a bounded text change; larger or incompatible changes fall back to a bounded excerpt. Unchanged cached analysis is reused. Changing language processes eligible documents lazily, not the whole library on startup.
- **Defaults and consent.** New installations enable document chat and automatic analysis by default, but cloud requests wait for completed setup and a connected OpenRouter account. Existing saved choices are preserved. Settings explains that requests send document content and spend account credits; chat and background analysis can be disabled separately.
- **Per-format default reader.** Choose supported file types independently in the wizard or later in **Settings → Settings & AI…**.

### Collection and tab groups

- **Dynamic or fixed storage.** New setup defaults to a dynamic budget of 80% of free space plus existing Collection storage. Adjust it from 50% to 80%, select a static limit, or choose Unlimited. Existing storage choices remain intact. Cleanup affects saved history, not original files; kept history and editable working documents remain protected.
- **Move location.** Collection files are transferred and verified before old stored data is removed. Small routing records keep existing tabs usable. When editable Collection documents exist, moving to another folder on the same disk is supported; a move to a different disk is refused to protect in-progress saves.
- **Missing originals remain readable.** Available Collection content opens in the same tab and group with the reading position preserved. A red status pill and rounded **Locate…** button share the existing toolbar, with a compact layout for narrow windows. Locate offers automatic exact-content recovery or manual selection. This also covers unreadable protected sources, damaged originals and repeated loss of access after reconnecting.
- **Editable Collection documents.** Open and rename saved copies without losing their original source references or version history. Working files are separate from deduplicated snapshots; saved changes join the same history and unchanged bytes do not add a revision. Merely opening an older version does not make it latest.
- **Original groups, not a Backups group.** Collection copies use their original document groups. Legacy Collection Backups tabs migrate to a matching source group, or General when no source tab is available. Recovery reuses an already-open source tab instead of creating a duplicate.
- **Drop between groups.** A tab dropped in a gap joins General. If General does not exist, it is created at that position; an existing General keeps its position and is revealed if hidden. This works between collapsed groups and across windows. The insertion marker and final mouse position determine the result, preventing a previously hovered group from capturing the drop.

## For AI integrations

These additions belong to the macOS **in-reader OpenRouter assistant**. They are separate from the existing CLI/stdio MCP interface; a chat tool name does not imply a new external MCP command. See the [document assistant guide](../document-assistant.md) and [AI control and Markdown authoring guide](../agent-interface.md).

- **Discover context before reading files.** Workspace discovery pages through groups and tabs using opaque IDs without opening their files. `document_metadata` returns cached summary, keywords, tags and chapters. An explicit chat send includes the active document ID, title and page.
- **Bounded evidence tools.** Text reads return up to 20 pages per call. Cross-document search checks up to 16 open documents and 100 pages per document, returning at most 50 matches with coverage/continuation information. Page text reads do not attach images automatically; `render_page` and `current_view` are explicit image requests.
- **Full-page and region images.** `render_page` accepts a page, optional revision, optional normalized top-left region, and a longest edge of 256–2400 pixels (1600 by default). It returns dimensions, the exact region and a revision-checked citation. Images use original colors; a whole-page overview can remain beside a detail crop. Saved previews are replayed as image evidence, subject to four-image/9 MiB PNG limits per request. Text-only models and unavailable pixels are reported explicitly.
- **On-demand viewport evidence.** `current_view` returns document-only pixels, reading controls, visible page regions, selected text and exact source citations. It includes live Markdown layout identity. It is lazy, cancellable, bounded, and does not navigate or capture other apps.
- **Structured final answers.** `present_answer` accepts a single-line `label` (up to 80 characters), `value` with units (up to 48), and an exact retrieved `citation`. Called alone after evidence retrieval, it records the card and finishes without another answer-generation round. Invalid values or unverified citations return an error; presentation metadata stays local.
- **Optional revision compatibility.** `read_pages`, `render_page` and `show_document` accept an empty optional revision as omitted, avoiding false document-changed errors and needless retries from some providers. Nonempty stale revisions and malformed types still fail; document edits retain their required revision checks.
- **Source navigation.** Citations carry document, page, revision and relevant layout identity; text-search links retain exact highlight text. A malformed highlight on an otherwise verified page can fall back to that page. Unverified page/document links remain blocked. Provider reasoning state survives tool continuations within a turn but is neither displayed nor saved to chat history.
- **Workspace and reader actions.** Tools support group creation, naming, ordering, visibility/collapse, moving tabs, supported reader controls, navigation, eligible PDF comments and rotation, tab close/reopen, writable-file and Collection-working-copy rename, Collection discovery/opening, and requested OCR/translation/PDF jobs. Mutations return receipts and check revisions/state before committing.
- **Generated metadata editing.** `update_document_metadata` edits cached generated chapters, summary, keywords, up to six tags and analysis language with Undo/Redo. It preserves authored headings/bookmarks and validates page anchors against analyzed evidence.
- **Chat organization.** `list_chats` returns paged titles, folders and optional matching message context. `organize_chats` applies up to 200 operations atomically: create folders, move chats, rename chats and rename folders. An organization revision protects concurrent edits; a failed batch changes nothing.
- **Native app access.** `app_controls` discovers menus and visible controls, activates commands and edits supported values. Secure/credential fields are excluded. Queued dispatch is distinguished from confirmed completion; ordinary confirmation/file/permission dialogs remain in place. Generic native commands report `undoable: false`.
- **Revision metadata and lazy diffs.** Listing Collection revision IDs, dates, sizes and diff status reads metadata only. Requesting a revision diff uses saved text indexes, not archived document bytes: at most 30 pages, 128,000 combined text characters and 4 MiB per index. Results explicitly mark partial/unavailable coverage; they do not measure image or full-file binary changes. Compatible background updates send previous analysis plus at most 32,000 changed UTF-16 units.
- **Offline CLI/MCP grouping.** Organizing saved tabs while the app is closed stays within its locked session-YAML transaction. It does not start asynchronous live-reader Collection updates or write unrelated document state.
- **Published model comparison.** The [six-model benchmark](../journals/ai-reader/model-benchmark-2026-10-11.md) includes 60 fresh attempts, independent grading, source/citation checks, measured latency and recorded provider cost. The [opt-in reusable runner](../../../tools/ai-model-benchmark/README.md) uses the production chat/retrieval pipeline with isolated read-only fixtures; it is separate from the app and makes paid requests only when explicitly run. Public results exclude private documents, transcripts and credentials.
- **Privacy and limits.** Requests require OpenRouter no-data-collection and zero-data-retention routing, with no weaker-privacy retry. Credentials stay in Keychain. Tools have no shell, web browser or other-app access. Chat has bounded messages/context and up to 12 model/tool rounds per send; automatic analysis allows 20 uncached requests per reader session, sampling up to 30 pages/64,000 characters and skipping locked, textless or over-256 MiB files. Images are generated only for explicit image-tool requests, with no new image work at launch or document opening.

## Availability and limitations

- The new assistant and setup experience are macOS features. A connected OpenRouter account and credits may be needed. The app does not impose a chat-spending cap; model/provider availability and pricing can change.
- Page-image inspection needs an image-capable model. Scans without extractable text are not automatically summarized. OCR/translation jobs need installed local dependencies; supported writable-file edits retain their documented size and format limits.
- Source buttons verify that a citation was retrieved and remains valid. They do not certify the model’s interpretation. In the [60-attempt comparison](../journals/ai-reader/model-benchmark-2026-10-11.md), supported-answer passes were 8/10 for DeepSeek, Haiku, Luna and GLM, 6/10 for MiMo and 0/10 for Gemini Flash Lite. Applying the stricter whole-page retrieval requirement lowers Haiku and Luna to 6/10; every model failed the reported drawing question twice. This small sample does not establish general model accuracy. DeepSeek remains the default; this release does not claim the drawing-interpretation problem is solved or a measured response-speed improvement.
